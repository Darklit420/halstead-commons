Questions

Before you
sit down.

Do I need an appointment?⌄
Booking is the safer bet. One chair means one person at a time. Walk-ins get taken when the chair is free, but you may be waiting.
How long does a cut take?⌄
About 45 minutes for a cut, an hour if you are having the beard done as well. We do not rush and we do not double-book.
What if I do not like it?⌄
Come back within a week and we will sort it. That is not a policy we advertise to be nice. It is easier for everyone than you living with a bad cut.
Do you cut kids' hair?⌄
Yes. Under 12s are $22 any style. Bring them earlier in the day if they do not like waiting.
