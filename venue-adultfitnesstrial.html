⭐ About the Class

Two hours, your own station, and everything provided down to the apron. You cook one dish properly and eat it at the end. Most people arrive on their own and leave talking to the person next to them.

✅ What You'll Experience
🔥
One Technique, Done Properly
You leave with a technique you'll actually use again, not a recipe you follow once. Most people cook the dish at home within the week.
⚡
Built Around One Technique
Our sessions are designed around High Intensity Interval Training, consistently keeping your heart rate up for outstanding results and plenty of enjoyment.
🔪
One Technique A Class
Knife work, braising, bread, pasta. Learn one properly and it carries into everything else you cook.
🍽️
Eat What You Make
An open kitchen night for anyone who wants to practise without a set menu. Bring an idea, use the space.

Questions

Before your
free class.

What if I cannot make Wednesday?⌄
Email us and we will find you a spot on the next class. The free class is not tied to that one date.
Do I need any experience?⌄
None. The class is built for people who have not done this before as much as people who have.
What should I wear?⌄
Something you do not mind getting a bit of oil on. Closed shoes — the kitchen has that rule for everyone.
Is there a catch after the free class?⌄
No. You can join monthly or drop in whenever, or just say thanks and leave. Nobody will chase you.
