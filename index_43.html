About Us

An old mill, still working.

Halstead Commons is a converted mill on Foundry Street with ten independent businesses across three floors. A gym in the old boiler room, a teaching kitchen, a pottery and woodwork studio, a barber, an early learning room, and more.

Every tenant runs their own business their own way. We keep the lights on, the freight elevator moving, and the shared floors open to everyone who works here.

Questions

Things people
usually ask.

Who actually runs the building?⌄
Halstead Commons is managed on site, not by an agency somewhere else. Marcus handles the building, Dana is on the front desk six days a week, and Carol deals with leasing. You will meet all three.
Can I just walk in and look around?⌄
Yes. The ground floor and shared landings are open Mon–Sat 6AM–9PM and Sunday 9AM–6PM. Individual tenants keep their own hours. Most are happy for you to put your head round the door.
Do the businesses here work together?⌄
Sometimes. Camp Halstead runs its focus blocks with the kitchen, the pottery studio and the workshop downstairs. Offhours holds its end-of-season social in The Loft. Mostly though, ten businesses share an address and a freight lift.
Is there parking?⌄
Six spaces behind the building on Foundry Street, first come first served. Street parking is free after 6PM and all day Sunday. The loading bay is for deliveries only.
